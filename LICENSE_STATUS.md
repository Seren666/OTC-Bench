# Component rights and licensing status

The project owner authorized public distribution to the OTC-Bench GitHub and Hugging Face repositories on 2026-09-30. **No specific author-owned code/metadata reuse license has been selected yet.** Suggested decisions are MIT for newly authored code and CC BY 4.0 for author-owned benchmark metadata. These are recommendations, not already granted licenses; public availability alone does not grant those terms.

COCO's official terms separate annotations from photographs:

- COCO annotations are licensed CC BY 4.0; retain COCO attribution and identify our geometry/assignment modifications.
- COCO does not own image copyright. Source photographs retain their original terms. `data/sources.jsonl` retains each recorded license and COCO/Flickr reference.
- Among the 816 source images (800 targets plus 16 donors), recorded licenses include 281 with NoDerivs clauses and many noncommercial clauses. A transformed-image upload cannot simply inherit our preferred metadata license. Recorded COCO license IDs are historical metadata, not a fresh certification of current rights.

Therefore this candidate contains **no original or transformed photographs**. It supplies source references, hashes and reproducible construction. Users must obtain sources and assess their permitted use. This approach does not eliminate users' obligations for derivative images. Any later image pack or public photo examples need a source-specific rights/attribution decision.

Only aggregate human vote counts are included. Raw questionnaires, names, click coordinates, timestamps and free-text reasons are excluded. Public release authorization is not evidence of a particular participant-consent procedure or institutional determination. No consent, ethics approval or exemption is asserted by this package.

Official COCO terms checked 2026-09-30: https://cocodataset.org/#termsofuse (content: https://cocodataset.org/dataset/termsofuse.htm).
