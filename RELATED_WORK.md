# Related-work boundary, checked 2026-09-30

This is a bounded novelty check, not proof that no equivalent work exists. arXiv primary records were re-fetched for 11 papers. Full HTML was inspected for O-Bench, Idis, DistractMIA, RIO-VQA and AutoHallusion. OpenAlex discovery queries covered VLM occlusion, semantic distractors and identity substitution, but retrieval was noisy and cannot establish exhaustive coverage. Previous search notes are supporting history, not fresh verification of every claim.

| Closest work | Verified focus | What this release contributes beyond that focus |
|---|---|---|
| [O-Bench: Beyond the Visible](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04059) | 1,365 constructed images, 4,588 questions across five occlusion-perception tasks; SA-1B layered synthesis. | A narrower specified-target diagnostic with identical donor pixels exchanged between overlap and near positions while visible target evidence stays fixed; donor-directed answer transitions are the outcome. Do not claim the first occlusion benchmark. |
| [Idis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21397) | Visual/linguistic distractors, semantic and numerical variation, reasoning-length/accuracy analysis; target information and answer preserved. | Fixed partially occluded target evidence and a matched ordinary-object donor exchange; open answers are compared to the specific donor identity. General distractor robustness is prior art. |
| [DistractMIA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12574) | Semantic distraction for black-box membership inference; optimizes distractor configuration and uses distractor-mention rate in an output-based membership score. | Specifies an original target, freezes ordinary object crops without model-guided stimulus search, and pairs overlap/near placement. **Neither inserting a semantic patch nor counting donor mentions is independently new.** |
| [AutoHallusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10900) | Abnormal/paired object insertion and correlated-object removal to induce contextual hallucinations. | Same-donor spatial exchange and unchanged remaining target pixels, with per-event target/donor/UNKNOWN transitions. Synthetic adversarially difficult scenes alone are not novel. |
| [Read or Ignore? / RIO-VQA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11899) | Same-scene counterfactuals vary question intent and clean/attack text to separate reading from ignoring typographic content. | Non-text object-pattern donors; question intent stays fixed and spatial overlap changes. Counterfactual robustness protocols already exist. |
| [SADL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30393) | Subject-aware distractor localization for image editing (primary abstract checked). | Classifying a specified occluded original target rather than locating objects to remove. |
| [Missingness Bias in Model Debugging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08945) | Missingness interventions can themselves alter model behavior (primary abstract checked). | Motivates cautious interpretation: equal visible evidence and matched fill reduce some confounds but do not prove a propagation mechanism. |
| [Set-of-Mark Prompting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11441) | Visual markers support grounding (primary abstract checked). | The red rectangle here specifies the task; marking is not claimed as a new method. |
| [Seeing without Looking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22903) | Whether VLM benchmarks require visual evidence (primary abstract checked). | A localized target-versus-donor identity question under partial occlusion, not global vision-removal reliance. |
| [Patch is Enough](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04884) and [Patched Visual Prompt Injectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.10529) | Adversarial patch attack/defense settings (primary abstracts checked). | Donors are frozen recognizable object crops, not optimized against these models. Do not claim general adversarial-patch novelty. |

## Candidate contribution statement

We provide a controlled, reproducible benchmark of spatially conditioned donor-category substitution in specified-target recognition under artificial object-pattern occlusion. The core unit is a paired exchange preserving the donor pixels and remaining visible target pixels. Evaluation separates target recognition, directed donor answers, other errors and abstention, and reports model-specific behavior with human-readability corroboration.

Evidence supports a diagnostic protocol and benchmark contribution. It does not yet explain architectural or training causes, demonstrate a repair method, establish natural-scene prevalence, or prove a general law across VLM families. Existing token-blocking and marker experiments did not establish a reliable repair and are not the central contribution.

The central remaining novelty risk is an equivalent target-specific same-patch overlap/near swap in unreviewed literature. Re-run a narrow citation-neighborhood search immediately before submission.
